AI provider — run the twin on a real LLM, our extension way

      
Point MarketerTwin at any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, Gemini-compat proxies, LM Studio…). The extension sends the pre-made sales-master system prompt below plus your retrieved memory — the model answers as your twin. Without a provider, the full built-in engine answers (5,148 chat experiences, 80 marketing topics, 56 sales tactics, 6 books) — nothing breaks, ever.
