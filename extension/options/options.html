Capture

The exclusion check runs before any script touches a page, so blocked content is never written down — not even briefly.

Sites — what the AI is allowed to read

Every site you have visited is listed here. Pick a mode per site:

  • Remember captured, and the AI may use it to answer.
  • Hide from AI the link is saved so you can find it, but the content is never shown to the AI.
  • Block nothing is recorded at all — and anything already stored for that site is deleted.

Knowledge growth (backend web search)

Optional. When on, the backend may search the web about topics you already read about, and notify you when something genuinely new turns up. Every outbound query is logged and shown in the dashboard so you can audit it.

Your AI brain (on-device)

The whole brain now lives inside the extension: your pages are chunked, indexed and answered from this browser only. No server needed, no internet needed, works offline. The Flask backend is just an optional mirror for the dashboard.

How it stays honest

  • Answers use only the pages retrieval actually returned — never the model's imagination.
  • Every claim carries a title, a site and a date, so you can click through and check.
  • When nothing matches, the answer is “I don't have anything in your history matching that.”
  • Pages the backend fetched itself are labelled assistant-fetched and are never described as something you read.
Twin-Brain Local-first by design: your memory lives in a SQLite file on your own machine.
